# Design research

Use for new journeys, substantial redesigns, or unclear visual direction. Do not research externally for a small edit that clearly reuses an approved design system.

## Source order

1. Existing project `DESIGN.md`, UI, and supplied references.
2. Official platform design and accessibility guidance.
3. Official Mobbin MCP when connected.
4. Approved `DESIGN.md` reference libraries.
5. Current web research and primary product sources.

When Mobbin is unavailable, say so and continue with other sources. Never claim a Mobbin search without tool evidence.

Research by product category, journey, platform, interaction, and emotional tone. Extract principles such as hierarchy, sequencing, disclosure, recovery, feedback, and motion. Do not copy a distinctive screen, brand identity, illustration, or exact wording.

Write `docs/design-research/<feature>-research.md` with sources, observed patterns, why they work, accessibility/motion findings, rejected directions, originality boundaries, and a recommended direction.

Read `references/research-sources.md` for source and safety guidance.
