# Refine result

Convert feedback into: target, keep, change, do not change, visual annotations, and verify. Use the bundled template.

Identify the exact revision and artifact under review. Map each annotation or recording timestamp to a concrete request. Resolve conflicts with the approved master plan before editing. Preserve approved architecture and unrelated work. Apply the smallest complete change and rerun the affected checks plus nearby regression risks.

Report the annotation-to-change mapping, preserved behavior, verification, and remaining subjective choices. Do not use “make it better” as an unbounded redesign instruction.
